Plans near you

Estimates, not quotes. Yearly cost = 12 months of premiums plus estimated drug costs for a full calendar year. Drug costs follow 2027 Medicare drug rules: the plan's deductible, its copays or coinsurance, a $2,400 yearly limit on covered drug costs, and insulin at no more than $35 a month. Drugs a plan doesn't cover are priced in full and flagged. Doctor visits, hospital stays and other medical costs are not included; each plan's yearly maximum out-of-pocket for medical care is shown.

Drug prices. Copays come from 2027 plan data. Coinsurance is applied to each plan's latest posted drug prices (2026 Q3), with CMS's negotiated 2027 prices applied to the 15 newly negotiated drugs. These will be updated when CMS posts 2027 prices.

Sources. CMS 2027 Medicare Advantage Landscape; CMS 2027 Part D formulary and cost-sharing files (October 2026); CMS 2026 Q3 Part D pricing file; CMS 2027 Star Ratings (October 8, 2026); NLM RxNorm drug names; Census ZIP-to-county file; doctor networks from the provider directories insurers file with Medicare (machine-readable files and public FHIR directory APIs); doctor search from the CMS NPPES national provider file. Always confirm plan details on Medicare.gov, at 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227), or with the plan before you enroll.

Private by design. Your ZIP code, drugs and doctors stay in this browser. Nothing is sent to us or stored on a server. Medicare4Me does not sell insurance or enroll you in a plan.
